# Commercial & Video Composition Blueprints

This reference catalogs the composition templates available in the Remotion engine for client video production.

---

## 1. Commercial Blueprint (60s Master Ad)
- **Resolution**: 1920x1080 (Landscape) or 1080x1920 (Vertical 9:16)
- **Framerate**: 60 FPS
- **Duration**: 3600 frames (60.0s)

### Scene Breakdown:
1. **Scene 1: Hook (0s – 5s / Frames 0–300)**:
   - High-energy kinetic typography (`split-words` spring reveal)
   - Dynamic dark gradient background with breathing pulse
   - SFX: Sub-bass drop + subtle riser
2. **Scene 2: Problem / Tension (5s – 18s / Frames 300–1080)**:
   - B-roll video clip with desaturated cinematic grade
   - Problem statement in staggered bold typography
   - Background music enters under voiceover
3. **Scene 3: The Solution & 3D Mockup (18s – 38s / Frames 1080–2280)**:
   - 3D Device Mockup (iPhone/MacBook) floating with physical reflections
   - Feature bullet callouts popping in with tactile clicks
   - Floating particle dust VFX
4. **Scene 4: Proof & Metrics (38s – 48s / Frames 2280–2880)**:
   - Large animated metric counter (e.g. `+340% ROI`)
   - Testimonial card with brand lower-third
5. **Scene 5: Call to Action & Logo Reveal (48s – 60s / Frames 2880–3600)**:
   - Animated SVG logo draw-on with metallic glow
   - Primary action URL / QR code
   - Final musical resolve and audio fade-out

---

## 2. Product Showcase Blueprint (30s)
- **Duration**: 1800 frames (30.0s at 60 FPS)
- **Focus**: Highlighting a physical or digital product's design, ergonomics, and key feature loop.
- **Visual Style**: Apple Keynote style minimalism, high-contrast typography, slow rotating 3D models.

---

## 3. Brand Reel / Social Cut (15s Vertical 9:16)
- **Resolution**: 1080x1920
- **Duration**: 900 frames (15.0s at 60 FPS)
- **Focus**: Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts.
- **Visual Style**: High-velocity cuts (every 1.5–2 seconds), large karaoke subtitles in center safe area.
